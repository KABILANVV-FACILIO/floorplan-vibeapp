# Floorplan Manager

## Use case

The app is built for ENEC's workplace and facilities teams, who run their sites on Facilio. They need to see, on the real floor plan, where every desk, locker, parking stall, room and delivery area is, who holds it, and what is booked. Before this app, assignments, bookings and floor layouts lived in separate Facilio modules and lists, with no plan that tied them together.

The app runs inside ENEC's Facilio — embedded in the web app as a connected app, and in the Facilio mobile app — and as a standalone vibe app. Admins lay out floors (upload a plan, place markers, draw rooms), assign desks, lockers and stalls to employees, and configure modules, booking rules, access and first responders. Everyone else finds their desk, books a desk, room or stall for a time window, and sees who sits where. On a phone the same plan opens with pan/zoom, a QR scanner that jumps to a space, and booking sheets.

The org's first responders (first aiders, floor wardens and the org's own roles) are marked on the desks they hold, listed per floor, and printed on the floor's emergency notice.

## Business requirements

1. **R1** The plan for any floor in the org's portfolio (site → building → floor) can be opened, searched and navigated; the app reopens on the floor in the URL, else the viewer's own desk's floor. A floor that cannot be loaded says so — Facilio busy, or not loaded — and the plan goes back to the floor that was on screen; a failed Refresh keeps the floor as it was. A part of the app that fails to draw says so in its place, and the navigation and the rest stay up.
2. **R2** Desks, lockers, parking stalls (point markers) and rooms, delivery areas (polygon zones) are drawn at their real positions, coloured by state or by department.
3. **R3** An admin can upload a floor plan (image, PDF, DWG/DXF), place, move and delete markers, draw zones, and save; a save writes to the org's own floor-plan records, and nothing is reported "saved" that the org did not store. Deleting a desk asks for confirmation first. On an org floor a new desk is created in Facilio first: refused, nothing is added and the reason is said. While "Save changes" runs, the unsaved-changes dialog cannot be cancelled.
4. **R4** Assigning a desk creates the org's own Move; assigning a locker or stall writes its `employee` field. The plan shows the holder; "My desk" finds the viewer's desk, and the "Your desk" pin marks it — only the signed-in person's own desk, and only a desk; a parking stall they hold reads "Your parking", a locker nothing, and none until Facilio has said who they are. Vacating asks first, naming who comes off.
5. **R5** A desk, room or stall can be booked for a time window through the org's space booking (or facility booking, per Settings); the calendar lists the org's bookings and the viewer's own. A booking is made or cancelled in Facilio or not at all: with no Facilio host it is refused, and a cancel Facilio refuses says so — nothing is "booked" or "cancelled" in the browser alone. The plan reads only its floor's bookings; the calendar reads org-wide. The plan, its counts and its print open on the slot the org's clock is in now — on the pickers' 30-minute steps, one booking slot long, kept to the bookable 07:00–20:00 — not a fixed hour, so "booked" means booked now. Cancelling asks first, and the booking stays listed until Facilio has cancelled it. A clash check never passes on a part-read of the bookings; the calendar says when some bookings could not be loaded, or none (with Retry). A workflow step that needs a comment asks for it in the app. The calendar's "New booking" opens the form on the day in view at the next half-hour still to come (09:00 on a later day; tomorrow's first slot when today has none left).
6. **R6** Settings › Roles & access decides which roles see each tab and mode (map, assign, book, edit, bookings, people, settings); Settings always stays open to the org's admin roles, and is closed to a viewer whose role is unknown (the app's own URL outside Facilio). Only an admin, or a role the stored grid gives Settings, can change the grid, the responders, the other settings and the department colours — checked on the server against Facilio, which also records who did it. The print settings are the exception (R13): anyone who prints may change them.
16. **R16** Demo data (sample sites, people, desks, bookings) appears only under the local dev server — never in a deployed build, even when an org list comes back empty.
7. **R7** Settings › Modules switches any of the five modules off; a disabled module is hidden everywhere, never emptied, and the last one cannot be switched off (the window refuses it and says why).
17. **R17** Assigning and booking are rights in Settings › Roles & access, under Floorplans beside Edit, given role by role; both start with no one, admins included, and a viewer whose role is unknown gets them only when they are open to everyone. A view the role lacks is gone on the web and in the mobile app — its switch and its actions; without Assignment the People list stays, read-only (no dragging onto the plan), and without either right the plan shows the floor, its markers and the People list, with Edit (its own right), My desk, print and refresh. The Bookings tab keeps its own row, with its own right under it: Bookings › Book, booking by selecting a slot in the calendar (closed until granted, like the plan's). Without it the calendar is for looking: a press says so and starts no selection.
18. **R18** A click on a person in the People list beside the plan shows their space on the plan on screen — the desk, stall or room they hold, else one they have booked in the toolbar's window — as a click on it would (centred, selected, its card open); their employee record opens only from the small icon at the end of the row. The People list has a Department picker under its search: ticking departments narrows the list to their people, as the same Department filter the Filter panel offers. The Spaces panel keeps its own Department filter for spaces, a multi-select like the People list's (none ticked = every department): a space belongs to its own department, else its holder's; the ticked departments' spaces are listed, other departments' markers go faint and lose their labels, and a chip on the plan names the ticked departments and clears them. With any filter on, a line under the search says how many and clears them; the Filter panel opens in the list's place (not over it), so Clear all and Apply are reachable however short the list.
19. **R19** The People panel's Filter offers the org's employee fields with the operators of their type — Department, Line Manager, Designation, HRMS Employee ID, Site and Desk first — and a lookup's choices come from the org's picklist, searched as you type. Search matches name, email and the org's own HRMS Employee ID field.
20. **R20** A desk, a parking stall and a room can each be labelled on the plan by any text field of its own record (Settings › Desks / Parking / Rooms › Label on the plan) — a desk's Desk Code, a short "Stall Number" the org adds in Facilio, a room's Code — in place of the long record name, falling back to the name where the field is empty; for desks and stalls the assigned person (their name under the label, initials in the chip) can be hidden. Screen and print alike, and the Spaces search finds a unit by the label the plan shows. The app never adds a field: ENEC's admin does, and it is offered once it exists.
21. **R21** Every plan prints registers after it, each with its own choices in the print window's Print settings (a tab per kind, one Apply): desks and rooms after a desk plan, stalls after a parking plan, lockers after a locker plan. One row per space, a column per detail chosen — the space's record (stall: name, plan label, record ID, QR value, type, mode, status, area, location; desk: name, plan label, record ID, desk code, QR value, desk type, desk department, status, room, location; locker: name, record ID, status, room, location; room: name, plan label, record ID, code, bookable, status, location), the assigned person's employee record (name, HRMS ID, department, designation, grade, mobile, email, line manager) — or for a room its booking in the date and hours picked in the toolbar (booked by, time, purpose) — and a stall's dates (occupied since, released on); all / assigned (booked) / vacant (free), sorted by name, status, department or person. A dash where the record is empty. Choices are kept for everyone. Parking prints its register by default, as before; desks, rooms and lockers only once turned on.
22. **R22** In the plan's top bar (Assignment and Booking views, desktop) an Occupancy chip reads one line — "Desks 91/122 · Rooms 0/18", taken of all per type — and covers no desk at any zoom (a floating card over the plan hid desks; in the Location panel it was out of sight). Hovering it opens a card under it; a click keeps the card open until a second click, Escape or a click elsewhere. The card counts every desk, parking stall and room placed on the floor, on any of its plans, in the state colours set in Settings: each type as its name with how many in all and how much is taken (all but the last tile), then three tiles lined up across the types — desks Occupied · Temporarily vacant · Vacant, stalls Occupied · Reserved · Vacant (the middle tile is the record's own state, read with the holders; desks and stalls are not booked in this org), rooms Occupied · Booked · Free for the date and hours picked in the toolbar, said under the title only when there is a Rooms row (a zero as a dash; a room, never held, shows Occupied as one). A held desk or stall is Occupied whatever its state says. A desk nobody holds that its record keeps Temporarily Vacant, and a stall kept Reserved, say so in their pill, tooltip and card too, in the vacant colour. Ticked departments narrow desks and stalls; rooms stay whole-floor and say so. In Assignment view a Status / Department switch colours the desks by department for the person looking only (Settings › Desks › Colour desks by stays the default for everyone): the plan and the legend take each department's colour, and under the desks' tiles the card shows their shares in a bar and the five largest departments, the rest behind "Show all". The colour pick is remembered in the browser. While the pick is set, Settings' Colour desks by changes nothing for that person until they flip it back.
8. **R8** Department colours, state colours, desk label style and booking slot granularity are configurable and shared org-wide. They are kept in the app's own database, where only admins and roles given Settings can change them; once stored there, no browser can overwrite them. Several department colours changed at once are saved together. A setting that could not be saved says why.
9. **R9** First responders: Settings › First responders names people from the employee directory under responder roles. Nothing about a person is typed in — their name, department and mobile are read off their employee record. The summary row of each role shows three names and "+N others", a button that lists everyone (touch and keyboard alike); Edit lists them all. Names show without the employee number in front.
10. **R10** Responder roles: first aider and floor warden are built in and cannot be removed; the org adds its own, each with a badge (symbol, shape, colour). A removed role comes off everyone who held it.
11. **R11** A responder is marked on the plan at the desk they hold on that floor (badges on the desk, up to four roles), listed in the floor's responder list (pick one to centre their desk), and printed on the floor's notice page (name, mobile, department, desk), once per person with every desk they hold there; a role heading in its own colour, no first-aid sign (removed 2026-11-17). Someone with no desk on a floor is not shown there.
12. **R12** Who holds which responder role is stored in the app's own database, never as new fields or configuration in the Facilio org.
13. **R13** The floor prints as a sheet: the plan, a legend, the parking register after a parking plan (R21), and the responder notice as the last page when the floor has responders. With departments ticked on screen (R18) the print is theirs: other departments' spaces faded and unlabelled, a Departments box beside the floor's name, the counts, the detail pages and the register theirs only, and the footer says so. The PDF carries the app's own font (Roboto, self-hosted), so it matches the preview. "Floor + details" cuts zoomed detail pages for the plan's own spaces — desks, or the stalls of a parking plan, or the lockers of a locker plan — so a car park whose stalls sit too close for their holders on the whole floor prints them all. What the plan's page shows — Floor + details (the default), Current view or Whole floor — is chosen in the print window's Print settings, staged with the registers' choices under one Apply and kept for everyone (anyone who prints may change them; they are stored in the app's own database); the preview's subtitle names it. The preview zooms: − and + (40% of the fitted page up to 4× life size), the percentage to fit the page again, a trackpad pinch or Ctrl + scroll, and Ctrl/⌘ +, − and 0 — around the point under the pointer, and scrolling both ways once zoomed past the fit.
14. **R14** On a phone (Facilio mobile app) the plan opens, pans and zooms, and a scanned space QR code opens that space — or says why it matched none or several. My bookings on a phone names the day picked, never the evening before on a device west of Greenwich.
15. **R15** Every action is reachable by keyboard; dialogs trap focus, close on Esc (innermost first) and return focus to what opened them. The People rows, the date picker and picklists, the Print settings choices (arrow keys, one Tab stop per group) and the first-responders list (focus in on open, back to the chip on Esc) included.

## Solution implemented

- **Screens and flows:** four views — Floorplans (`/`), Bookings, People, Settings — plus a mobile layout. Floorplans has a Location panel (portfolio tree, floor search, spaces list, filter chips), the plan canvas with legend and responder chip, the Assignment / Booking / Edit modes, an inspector panel, a print viewer, and a floating toolbar (Edit, My desk, print, refresh). Settings tabs: Roles & access, Modules, Bookings, First responders, Desks, Lockers, Parking, Rooms, Delivery areas.
- R1 → `PortfolioTree` + floor search (typed text debounced 300 ms, `searchFloors` answers kept 30 s); `urlFloor` reads/writes `?floorId=`; boot picks URL floor, then `fetchMyDesk`'s floor. A failed load: `SELECT_FLOOR_FAILED` (a switch goes back to the previous floor, a Refresh keeps its data, boot clears the skeleton) and `floorLoadFailedMessage` ("Facilio is busy — try again in a minute" / "Couldn't load that floor — try again in a moment"); stale answers are dropped by floor id (`ASSIGNMENTS_LOADED`, `SET_DATE`, `SET_PLAN`, `SET_FLOOR_IMAGE_LOADING`) and a date pick's sequence number. `ErrorBoundary` around the view switch, the plan drawing, `MobileApp`, `BookingModal` (closes the form), `PrintSheet` and `PrintViewer` (silent); each resets when its key changes.
- R2 → `Canvas` / `Marker` / zones; `markerModel` resolves colour by state or department.
- R3 → Edit mode (`EditPanel`, `FloorUploadModal`, `DeleteConfirmModal`); `persistUnits` writes `floorplanmarker` / `floorplanmarkedzone` / `indoorfloorplan` and reports exactly what the org stored. A new desk on an org floor goes through `FacilioApiDataSource.createUnit` directly (no local fallback): a refusal toasts "Not created in Facilio — {reason}". The local tier never keeps an org floor's units (with the API configured, a numeric floor's `saveUnits` resolves and stores nothing). Marker and zone syncs read the floor's lists strictly — a later page refused means nothing is written (no second copies). `ensurePlanGeoreference(floorId, planId, dims, { write })`: someone with `canEdit` writes as before (`write: true`); anyone else `write: 'fitted'` — only a quad fitted to markers placed in Facilio's editor, never a synthetic one; no quad is written when the plan's markers cannot all be read. `UnsavedChangesModal`: Cancel disabled while Save runs, the switch goes ahead when it lands.
- R4 → `assignEmployeeToRecord` (desk → `createDeskMove`, locker/stall → `employee` field update); `fetchMyDesk`. The pin's text is `selectors.myPinText` ("Your desk" / "Your parking", carried as `MarkerModel.pinText` and `MarkerLabelInput.pillText`); My desk = `myAssignedUnit`, the DESK held by `state.bookBy` (the account's people id; `''` until boot reads it) — desks only, since ENEC's admin account (people #28483) holds parking stalls and wore the pin on them.
- R5 → `BookingModal`, `createRealBooking` (`spacebooking`), Bookings calendar via `fetchOrgBookingsForRange`. The standalone booking-form connected app moved to its own repo (see `standalone/booking-forms/README.md`); only its TSX variant, `standalone/booking-forms/bookingForms.tsx`, stays here, outside this app's build — a booking rule changed here is mirrored there. `fetchOrgBookingsForRange(…, { strict })`: the calendar, the form's clash line and `submitBooking`'s clash check read strictly — a later page that fails rejects with the rows it got (`IncompleteListError`; "Some bookings could not be loaded"), a first page refused for rate with `RateLimitedError` ("Couldn't load bookings · Retry"); the floor load reads non-strict. `cancelBooking` resolves true or false (the calendar preview closes only on true); `ConfirmDialog` before every Cancel and Vacate (`StateflowActions`, `LocalAssign`, `MobileUnitSheet`, `BookPanel`, `BookingsView`, `MobileMyBookings`, `BookingStateflowActions`); `PromptDialog` for a transition that needs a comment (`details/transitionComment.ts` escapes it for the HTML body). Booking cards read their workflow actions only when "Actions" is pressed. Every surface reads a record's workflow buttons through `stateflowApi.readFlow(module, id, version)`: surfaces asking for the same record and version at once share one request, an answer is reused for 5 s (the plan card and the side panel open on one desk make one request), a transition drops what is held, and a failure is never kept. The version is the record's own (`state.recordVersions[id]`, bumped by `RECORD_CHANGED { ids }` for the records a write changed) with the plan-wide `recordNonce` for a change whose records are unknown — so a desk's buttons and card re-read when THAT desk changes, not on every assign anywhere on the plan. A booking action taken in the calendar re-reads the calendar, and the plan's day only when the plan is the view on screen or its day holds that booking; in API mode the calendar range is not re-read when the plan's floor changes. The Bookings site filter reads a ticked site's floors in one request (`getSiteFloors`: one `floor` list filtered by site, grouped by building; per building, two at a time, outside a Facilio host or when that list fails for anything but rate); a failed read says "Couldn't load floors · Retry" (never cached as no floors), and an answer landing after the popover closed ticks nothing. A booking save that throws comes off hold, says so, and the form can be closed; one with no answer after 60 s comes off hold too ("The booking has not been confirmed yet. Check the calendar before booking again."). `submitBooking` and `cancelBooking` call `refuseOrgWrite` first; inside Facilio `cancelBooking` calls `cancelRealBooking` (the record's Cancel transition) and toasts the refusal instead of falling through to the browser store. The plan's `getBookings` scopes the query to the floor (`floorIds`), ids split 150 to a query — always by id, since an org-wide read of a day is capped by the whole org's bookings. A booking read asks for the bookings that OVERLAP the range (`bookingStartTime` between 0 and the range's end, `bookingEndTime` between the range's start and an open end — BETWEEN (20), the operator this read already sent), not every booking started in the 31 days before; a booking with no end time is not matched. Should the org refuse that shape (a first page failing for anything but rate), the read falls back to the old one — started in the range or the 31 days before it — and keeps to it for the session once that answers. At most 6 pages of 500: a 6th page that comes back full sets `stopped`, so a strict read says "Some bookings could not be loaded" instead of passing the first 3,000 off as all of them; the first page refused for rate is a `RateLimitedError` either way. The calendar's floor filter reads the chosen floors' record ids in ONE list per module filtered by `floor` "is" (36) (`floorFilterIds`, 500 a page, kept 60 s, only whole lists; a page naming a record on another floor means the filter was not applied, and the floors are then read one by one, as before — as they are when the list fails for anything but rate), and above 2 floors or 300 ids reads the range org-wide and keeps the rows on those records; an org-wide read that stops short (its 3,000-row cap, or a later page that failed for anything but rate) asks the chosen floors' records by id instead. Ticking a site reads its floors in one `floor` list filtered by `site` (`getSiteFloors`, pages of 200 as every module list, grouped by building; a row naming another site dropped). The opening window: `orgTime.slotNow(minutes, slotGranularity)` — `buildInitialState` from the device clock, then `SET_WINDOW_NOW` with `orgNow()` once `fetchOrgTimezone` answers (FloorplanContext boot).

The preview's zoom: `PrintViewer` (`fit` × `zoom`, `ZOOM_MIN` / `SCALE_MAX` / `ZOOM_STEP`; a non-passive wheel listener and Safari's gesture events on the grey area; the anchor re-applied in a layout effect; the sheet element memoised so a zoom re-renders only the frame).
- Chips: `labelLayout.chipScreenSize` holds `CHIP_PX` (24) where the spaces have room, but never more than `CHIP_FIT_OF_PITCH` (0.9) of the gap to the neighbours, down to `CHIP_MIN_PX` (10) — a car park zoomed out drew 24px chips over each other. That size follows the floor's spacing, `printAreas.chipPitch`: the median nearest neighbour, but no more than 1.25× the gap of its closest quarter of spaces — ENEC's Test Floor 1 (8 spaces: four 250–330px from anything, a column of four 50px apart) took the median 257px, so at 53% every chip drew 61px with the column 26px apart, chips and cards piled up, and its labels grew with them; it now draws 24px there, as its print does. An evenly laid floor (closest quarter within 80% of the median) keeps its median, and page cutting keeps `deskPitch`. For a stray pair, `labelLayout.chipSizeNear` gives a chip that would cover its own nearest neighbour (`printAreas.nearestDistances`) just under that gap instead — that chip alone, its own `--chip` on the marker (`Marker`'s `chipScale`) and its own size to the layout (`markerLabelInputs`' `chipSizeOf`); a chip that clears its neighbour, every chip on an evenly laid floor, keeps the floor's size. `PrintSheet.printChipSize` applies the same rules on paper, only ever shrinking (`--chip` on the plane, `chipSize` / `chipSizeOf` to `markerLabelInputs`). A label's tick to its chip is `--ink-500`, dark enough to read over the drawing's lines, on screen and paper alike. A long holder name — wider than its desk code and than 110px (`LONG_HOLDER_PX`, scaled with the type) — is set a size down (`LONG_HOLDER_SCALE` 0.85, `longHolderType`) on two balanced lines (`placement.subSmall` / `subLines`, `longerLineShare` for the width, `text-wrap: balance` in `Marker`), the card about as wide as the code; it is shortened to first + last name only past two such lines (`holderMaxChars`). Desks, lockers and stalls nobody holds read "Vacant" (their records' word: `STATE_DEFS`, `unitStatus.vacantWord`, the legend, the card); rooms and delivery areas keep "Free" (their records' states are Active / In-active). Detail pages: `DETAIL_TYPES` (workstation, parking, locker) and `DETAIL_NOUN` per plan.
- R14 → `MobileApp`, `MobileQrScanner` + `qrMatch.matchSpaceCode`.
- R15 → `Modal` (focus trap, open stack, initial focus), `keyboard.activateOnKey`, `planShortcutAllowed`, keyboard-operable markers and tree rows. People rows are buttons ("Show <name>'s <label> on the plan"); `DatePicker` (a dialog: focus in on open, arrow keys, back to the field on close) and `fds/Picklist` (arrows, Home, End, Escape); `PrintDetails` roving tabindex; `FloorResponders` focuses its first row and returns to the chip on Escape; a collapsed `FloatingPanel` is a button; fields labelled (`useId`); mobile inputs 16px and safe-area insets.
- **Out of scope / deferred:** a responder with no desk is on no plan; at most four role badges per desk. The file-store copies of the settings and the colours are still written after each database save, for the builds deployed before the move (production v66, preview v76); once every deployed build reads the database, those copies can stop. App open still reads the whole staff directory (an id-filtered read of a floor's holders is a larger change, not started). The `floorplanApi` function and its app-database tier (`VibeDbDataSource`) were removed (2026-11-12): the function ran DDL the app's login may not, so it was never deployed; on-plan geometry lives in the org's `floorplanmarker` records, and the settings, then in the file store, are now behind `floorplanResponders` (see Data flow (settings)).

## Technical details

- **Frontend:** React 18 + TypeScript (strict) + Vite 5. One `FloorplanProvider` (`src/state/FloorplanContext.tsx`) with a reducer (`src/state/reducer.ts`); routes in `src/lib/routes.ts`. Host detection in `src/lib/hostKind.ts`: `'mobile'` (React Native WebView bridge), `'iframe'` (embedded connected app) or `'none'`. The viewer is identified off the account payload (`v2/fetchAccount`, else `v2/account`): people id and role.
- **Facilio data & connections:**
  - Connected-app V3 (through the host bridge, `src/lib/facilioApi.ts` / `facilioApiDataSource.ts`) — first tier when inside a Facilio host. Reads: `floor`, `employee` (all pages, `mapEmployee` → name, department, email, HRMS id, mobile, designation, grade, line manager), `department`, `desks`, `spacebooking`, `asset`, `indoorfloorplan`, `v3/floorplan/getFloorplanDetailsByType`, `v2/forms`, `v2/filter/advanced/fields/employee`, `v3/picklist/{module}` (a lookup filter's choices: `search`, `page`, `perPage`, `default` → `data.pickList` `[{value, label, secondaryLabel}]`), `v2/setup/getRoles`, state transitions. An employee field's own `operators` in the filter-fields answer are only its extras (ENEC's Line Manager lists just "Logged In User"); the usual operators come from its data type (LOOKUP 36/37/1/2, STRING 5/3/4/7/1/2, BOOLEAN 15, ENUM 54/55, MULTI_LOOKUP 90/91/104/105). ENEC's HRMS field is `hrms_employee_id_employee`, its Line Manager `line_manager_employee` (→ `people`). Writes: `floorplanmarker`, `floorplanmarkedzone`, `indoorfloorplan` (create/update/delete), `moves` (desk assignment), `spacebooking`, `employee` field on lockers/stalls. The mobile host needs bare API paths (`bridgeApiPath`).
  - Connection `facilio-cmms` (`src/lib/connectorDataSource.ts`, via `vibe.executeAction`) — second tier, and the whole data layer outside a Facilio host: `list-sites`, `list-buildings`, `list-floors`, `list-employees`, `list-assets`, `list-custom-module-records`, `create-space`.
  - Local JSON (`src/data/*.json` + localStorage) — offline `npm run dev` only.
  - **Rate limit** (`src/lib/rateLimit.ts`): Facilio allows 100 calls a minute per user, org and request URI, in a fixed 60 s window; past it, HTTP 429 / error code 6 until the window resets (bmsconsole AccessRateInterceptor, facilio-framework RedisAPIRateLimiter). The bridge gives no `X-Retry-After`, so a refused read waits the whole window plus up to 5 s jitter. `fetchAllPaged` (employees, sites, buildings, floors) keeps the pages read before a refusal and throws `IncompleteListError` — with a `resume` (wait, then carry on from the refused page; at most 2) when refused for rate; `CompositeDataSource` passes that partial answer on rather than trying the next tier. `readRoster` (boot and Refresh) uses the people read (boot) or keeps the roster on screen (Refresh), says "Facilio is busy — trying again in a minute", finishes in the background, and says "Couldn't load people from Facilio — names may be missing" when it gives up or the read fails outright. `loadRelated` reads a big floor's remaining pages two at a time (`RELATED_PAGE_CONCURRENCY`), never all at once. A second refusal while the roster's finish waits (Refresh pressed) leaves it to the one already waiting.
    - **Other reads:** every GET over the bridge (`customGet`, so record reads and related lists too) goes through `readPatiently`: refused for rate, it says "Facilio is busy — trying again in a minute" (`onFacilioBusy` → toast) and asks again after the window, at most twice; anything else comes back at once. A related list's count is asked only when its first page comes back full (500 rows) — most floors' lists fit one page and cost no count — and then once (`once`), so it never holds up the first page; a floor over 500 records of a module waits one extra round trip for it. `fetchAll` is not retried there (the paged reads do their own), but a refusal still says busy.
    - **Writes:** a plan save's marker and room-outline writes are decided first, then sent by `pacedWrites`: two at a time, at most 80 started in any 60 s (one log for every save), so a small save goes out at once and a newly mapped floor of 150 desks waits part-way ("Facilio is busy — saving N more in a minute", via `persistUnits(…, { onWait })`). A write refused for rate anyway stops the sending; the rest wait out the window and carry on, at most twice; still refused, they are kept unsaved and named in the save toast, to be sent by the next Save.
    - **A floor that loads in part:** a plan's marker list, or a page of the floor's desk / locker / stall / space lists, that fails is noted (`takeFloorReadFailure`) and the app says "Part of this floor couldn't be loaded — Refresh to try again" (ahead of the room-outline notice).
    - **Never again at once:** a read refused for rate is not re-sent straight away in another form — the bookings search keeps its cancelled-state filter (and the states it learnt) instead of asking again unfiltered, the org's bookable-spaces pool stops at the refusal (a partial pool is not kept; a whole one 60 s), a refused picklist read is not re-asked. Per-floor id reads, spacebooking searches and booking-form definitions run two at a time (`rateLimit.mapPooled`).
    - **Whole or refused:** `fetchAllRelatedPaged` waits for the whole list; an incomplete one answers `{ error, list: null }` (code 6 kept for a rate refusal). Display reads pass `allowPartial` (`getBuildings`, `getFloors`, `findUnitIdForDeskRecord`); reads behind a write are strict (`syncMarkers`, `syncZones`, `ensureRealSpaceRecord`, `ensurePlanGeoreference`), and `floorRecordIds` never caches an incomplete list. The re-read of holders after a change (`getAssignments` without a listener) fails on any incomplete page, and the composite never hands it to another tier; `recordStates.clearRecordStates` runs at the start of each read. `RateLimitedError` (an `IncompleteListError`) is thrown when the plan-type read is still refused after its waits, so a busy Facilio never drops the app to a weaker tier; any other failure still falls through to the connector. For an org floor no tier answers in the org's place: the local tier refuses an org floor's units, holders, bookings and creates (`refuseOrgFloor`; `saveUnits` still resolves, a no-op), and when every tier fails the composite throws the FIRST tier's error. So a busy or failing Facilio leaves the screen as it was and says so — a day that could not be read keeps the day on screen ("Couldn't load the bookings for that day" / busy), a floor whose spaces could not be read is not opened (`failFloorLoad`), a holders re-read that failed keeps the holders — and is never shown as an empty day or empty desks. A floor whose spaces load but whose day's bookings do not (busy, or a role that may not read bookings) still opens, with no bookings and that notice (`readDayBookings`). A floor's lists or plan image that never answer count as failed after 45 s.
    - **Read once, not again:** a floor's plan image is kept per floor and plan type for the session (`fetchFloorplanImage`: the plan record id, file id and the image as a Blob or data URL, the newest 6 floors): a return to a floor asks nothing, a fresh object URL is made for every answer (so the provider's revoking never blanks a plan), and after Refresh (`invalidateOrgCaches`) the file is downloaded again only if its file id changed; an upload drops the floor's entry. `getUnits` notes the plans whose georeference quad it read as valid, and `ensurePlanGeoreference` returns at once for them — the plan record is read once a floor open, not twice (the note goes with any geometry write, an upload, or Refresh). A refused floor search throws `RateLimitedError` before the index fallback (one call, and "Facilio is busy — search again in a minute", never "No floors match"); with the org reachable, a search it could not answer otherwise says "Couldn't search floors — try again" — the local tier answers no org search with the demo seed's floors. Re-reads of records just written (holders by id after an assign or vacate, `fetchRecordsByIds`) go past the host page's cache: in connected mode `api.fetchAll` is answered by the host, whose API layer keeps every GET's answer for 5 s (facilio-api `queryCache`), so they take the raw request path (`facilioApi.fetchAll(…, { fresh: true })`, as record reads do). One-time reads (the org time zone, the viewer's role and people id, employee filter fields, module fields, booking form types, list and detail) keep only a usable answer: a refused or empty one is asked again the next time.
    - **By id, not one by one:** `fetchRecordsByIds` reads up to 50 records of a module in one list (`id` "is" (36)); an answer holding a row it did not ask for means the org ignored the filter, and the reader falls back to the old way rather than trusting it. A room-outline save checks all its new rooms in ONE `space` read (plus one per point module when the space module id is unknown) — or, filter ignored, each room on its own as before; a point-module read that fails leaves its rooms unoutlined ("could not be checked"). After an assign or vacate the holders of the units it touched are read by id (`getAssignmentsFor`), the floor's whole holders read only when that read itself fails (not for rate).
    - **Pacer and retries:** the write pacer always releases its slot (`finally`), waits on a thrown 429 as on a refused answer, and shares its start log across tabs (`BroadcastChannel`, opened when the module loads, a no-op where missing or refused). A tab whose write is refused tells the others until when it waits (`{ holdUntil }`, capped at a window and its jitter), and every tab's pacer waits that out once before its next send (`linkWriteHold`). `stateflowApi` falls back to PATCH only when the first try was not refused for rate, a 500–504 or a timeout. `v2/fetchAccount` and `v2/account` are read once a session; the desk-vacated re-check reads with `{ once: true }`.
  - Connector backoff (`connectorDataSource`): a list refused for rate or with a 5xx is asked again from the refused page after ≥ 60 s + jitter, at most twice, its pages kept; a floor's desk, locker and stall lists two at a time. Only the reads it names (`READ_ACTIONS`) are retried on a 5xx; every other action — `create-space` — only on a rate refusal (`withBackoff(…, { retryOn: 'rate' })`): a create that met a server error may have landed, and sent again it would make the space twice. (Moving the connector reads to a server function: later.)

Every write first asks Facilio who is calling — `facilio-cmms.get-current-user-profile` (run as the caller) for the role, `facilio-user-management.list-roles` for whether it is privileged — and allows an admin, or a role the stored grid's `settings` row (`fp_access`) grants — or, when `fp_access` holds no grid at all, the `access.settings` grant inside the stored `settings` row of `fp_app_setting` (no rule anywhere: admins only); `save-print-settings` asks only who is calling (one call, no role list) and takes any caller Facilio names. Colours: `storeDepartmentColors` → `saveDepartmentColorScheme` → `save-department-colors`, then the file copy (not applied, and "Couldn't save the department colours — only admins…" at once, for a viewer `canChangeSettings` refuses); colour saves wait until the source has been read (`colorReads`: the database's answer, and a file read that worked or a scheme in the database). A refusal toasts "Couldn't save the {what} — {reason}." (`notSavedNotice`); the change stays on screen and its part goes again with the next change — except department colours, which go back to what is stored (`REVERT_DEPARTMENT_COLORS`), as responders and the grid do. A colour change while the scheme is still being read says so ("still loading"), not that it could not be read. Refresh re-reads the colours from wherever they live this session; from the database, its answer's responders and grid are applied too. Without the right to change the settings (`canChangeSettings`, where the database keeps them) the Edit view offers no "New marker": the marker could never be stored. NOTE for rollout: with no Settings rule stored in the database, only admins can change settings, colours and markers — the Settings tab's open default is not a rule the function sees — so an admin saves Roles & access once with the Settings row set before non-admins can.
- **Data flow (responders):** Settings › First responders › Save → `actions.setFirstResponders` → reducer (sanitise, names from `state.employees`) → when `state.respondersInDb`, `sharedSettings.saveResponders` → `floorplanResponders.save-responders` → tables. A refused write shows a toast and re-reads the database. On boot `sharedSettings.loadSharedSettings` runs beside the settings load; once it answers, the database is the only source (`APPLY_SETTINGS` ignores the blob's responders and `settingsFromState` omits them). Badges/list/notice then derive from `respondersOnFloor` (desk holder ∈ responders, one entry per desk); the notice and the plan's responder count go by person (`peopleOnFloor`, `responderCountLabel` counts distinct people), so someone holding three desks is one floor warden, listed once with the three desks a line each. The notice's desk and mobile columns are as wide as the page's longest entry (up to 24 / 18 characters), name and department share the rest — a fixed 13% desk column cut "TEest Desk 2" to "TEest De…" and ENEC's own codes with it.
- **Preview vs production:** functions are created/built on preview (`<name>_<uuid>_preview`); promoting a deployment copies them to production. Preview and production share one database schema, so every change to it must keep the builds already deployed working (N-1): tables and handlers are added, never changed under an older build, and `get-settings` only gains keys (older builds ignore them).

- A change to `functions/floorplanResponders/code.ts` ships separately from the front end: `FACILIO_PROFILE=default facilio vibe fn update floorplanResponders --code functions/floorplanResponders/code.ts && FACILIO_PROFILE=default facilio vibe fn build floorplanResponders`, BEFORE `facilio vibe deploy` (the app lives in ENEC CAFM, the CLI's `default` profile — a bare `facilio` uses whatever profile is active and answers "not found"). A new table goes first of all, once: `FACILIO_PROFILE=default facilio vibe db import --file functions/floorplanResponders/tables/fp_app_setting.csv --table fp_app_setting` (an import always makes a new table and there is no drop — never re-import one that exists). In that order each step is safe for what is already deployed: the table is new, so nothing reads it until the function does; the function only gains handlers and `get-settings` keys, which older builds ignore; and a front end that meets a function or database without the table (`appSettingsRead` false) keeps the file store for the settings, as before. A front end that knows a surface the function does not (`map.assign`, `map.book`, `bookings.book`) has every grid save refused whole ("unknown surface"); one that asks for `list-roles` before the function has it falls back to `v2/setup/getRoles`, which needs the Facilio host.
- **Known gaps:** a non-admin caller being refused by the function is unit-tested with the runtime stood in for, not live (only an admin identity was available); the paced save's waits are seen only after deploy to preview (`list-roles` is unit-tested in `code.test.ts`); `fp_app_setting` and the three settings handlers are unit-tested only, until the table is imported and the function updated on preview — a `save-app-settings` carrying custom markers with inline images (up to 2,000,000 characters) is not yet tried against the runtime's own request size or `truncated` read limits; once this build has stored a part in the database, a change made to it in a build from before the move (production v66, preview v76: file store only) is not seen by this build — the copy written after each database save keeps those builds up to date the other way; the browser → `floorplanResponders` path inside a real vibe host, the refusal toasts and the hidden Settings tab are verified only after deploy to preview; DWG parsing not tested against a real DWG; R4/R5 org writes are exercised manually in the ENEC org, not automated; R19's `v3/picklist` reads and filter requests through the host bridge are checked by contract (bmsconsole V3PIckListAction) and by the connector, not yet from inside Facilio.
